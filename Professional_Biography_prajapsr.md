### AI / Software Engineering Co-op

**Knorr-Bremse / Bendix Commercial Vehicle Systems**
**January 2026 – July 2026**

My most recent co-op experience has allowed me to work at the intersection of **Generative AI, agentic systems, full-stack engineering, cloud architecture, document intelligence, and enterprise automation**.

One of my primary projects involved developing an **AI-powered engineering drawing annotation system** designed to automate portions of the First Article Inspection process. I designed an end-to-end workflow that processes mechanical engineering drawings, identifies relevant information, determines annotation locations, generates ballooned drawings, and produces structured outputs that can support FAI documentation.

The system incorporated a **LangGraph-based workflow**, Azure Blob Storage, Azure AI Document Intelligence, large language models, and PyMuPDF. A significant engineering challenge involved ensuring that annotations were positioned accurately and consistently. Rather than relying entirely on vision models to estimate coordinates, I incorporated OCR-based coordinate extraction and deterministic annotation-placement algorithms to improve reliability and traceability.

I also contributed to the development of an **enterprise AI agent platform** that provides AI-powered capabilities through Microsoft's Power Platform ecosystem. My work involved technologies such as **React, TypeScript, Python, Azure Functions, Azure AI Foundry, LangGraph, Microsoft Dataverse, Power Automate, and Server-Sent Events**.

One challenge involved overcoming execution-time limitations within an existing AI-agent environment. I helped develop an alternative LangGraph-based architecture that supported longer-running AI workflows while streaming results back to the application to maintain a responsive user experience. I also worked with architectures designed to support different large language model providers without tightly coupling the application to a single model.

In addition, I developed enterprise automation solutions using **Power Apps, Power Automate, React-based Code Apps, and custom integrations**. These projects included automating document and utility-bill processing, developing workflow applications, integrating enterprise systems, and designing solutions around organizational security, networking, authentication, and deployment restrictions.

This experience has strengthened my ability to translate business problems into technical solutions while collaborating with engineers, technical leaders, and business stakeholders. It has also given me practical experience with requirements gathering, software architecture, debugging, technical documentation, deployment, and developing applications within enterprise environments.
