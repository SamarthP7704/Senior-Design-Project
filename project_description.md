## Project Topic Area

### AI-Assisted Trading Journal and Behavioral Analytics Platform

Our senior design project will explore the development of an AI-assisted trading journal platform focused on trade tracking, performance analysis, risk management, and trading behavior. The project will investigate how AI can help identify trading patterns and provide useful insights to support better decision-making and discipline.
