## Co-op Work Experience

**January 2025 - May 2025**

I simulated more than ten adversary techniques across Windows and Linux environments using Caldera and Atomic Red Team. I also developed OSQuery-based detections mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, helping improve endpoint visibility.

My responsibilities included validating more than 50 detection rules against real telemetry, configuring event-logging pipelines, and integrating detection results into Splunk and ELK dashboards for real-time monitoring. This experience strengthened my technical skills in OSQuery, Caldera, MITRE ATT&CK, Splunk, Linux, Windows, security telemetry, and anomaly detection. It also developed my research, documentation, analytical thinking, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities. I collaborated with research and content teams to support the development of a cybersecurity training portal.
